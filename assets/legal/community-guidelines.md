# Flixscope Community Guidelines

_Last updated: April 14, 2026_

Flixscope is a space for people who love movies and shows to share honest, thoughtful recommendations. These Guidelines set the tone of the community. By using Flixscope, you agree to follow them.

## What Flixscope is for

- Sharing quick takes, reviews, and personal opinions about movies and shows.
- Finding people whose taste matches yours and following them.
- Discovering what's actually worth your time — not what's just popular.

## What is not allowed

We do not tolerate content or behavior that falls into any of the categories below. Violations may result in content removal, account suspension, or permanent ban, at our sole discretion.

1. **Hate speech.** Content that promotes hatred, discrimination, or violence against any person or group based on race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, or any other protected characteristic.

2. **Harassment and bullying.** Targeting, threatening, stalking, intimidating, or repeatedly contacting another user in an unwanted way. This includes sharing someone's private information without consent ("doxxing").

3. **Sexual content, nudity, or exploitation.** Pornographic material, sexually suggestive content, or anything that sexualizes minors in any form. Content that depicts or promotes child sexual abuse material is reported to the appropriate authorities without exception.

4. **Violence and graphic content.** Glorifying, promoting, or threatening violence. Depicting gore, self-harm, or suicide in a way that encourages imitation.

5. **Illegal activity.** Content that promotes illegal drugs, weapons trafficking, human trafficking, fraud, piracy of copyrighted works, or any other unlawful activity.

6. **Spam, scams, and manipulation.** Repetitive posts, bot behavior, fake engagement, phishing, pyramid schemes, crypto scams, or any attempt to deceive other users for personal gain.

7. **Impersonation.** Pretending to be another person, brand, or public figure. Parody and fan accounts are allowed only if clearly labeled as such.

8. **Intellectual property violations.** Posting content you do not own or have permission to share. Do not copy other users' takes word-for-word.

9. **Malware and exploits.** Posting links to malicious software, phishing sites, or content designed to damage devices or compromise accounts.

10. **Off-topic or irrelevant content.** Quick takes should be about movies and shows. Political manifestos, advertisements, personal grievances, or unrelated rants do not belong on Flixscope.

11. **Circumvention.** Using multiple accounts to evade a ban, manipulate ratings, or bypass these Guidelines in any way.

12. **Anything else that a reasonable person would find abusive, misleading, or harmful.** We reserve the right to remove content and restrict accounts for reasons not explicitly listed here when necessary to keep the community safe.

## How we enforce these rules

We review every report we receive, usually within 24 hours. Depending on the severity and context of a violation, we may:

- Remove the offending content without prior notice.
- Issue a warning.
- Temporarily suspend the account.
- Permanently ban the account and block re-registration from the same device or email.
- Report the user and content to law enforcement when the conduct involves illegal activity.

We may take action proactively when we become aware of violations, even without a report.

## How to report content

If you see a quick take, profile, or message that violates these Guidelines:

1. Tap the "⋯" menu on the content.
2. Select **Report**.
3. Choose the reason that best fits and submit.

You can also block any user directly from their profile. Blocked users cannot see your content, and you will not see theirs. Blocking is silent — the other user is not notified.

If the issue is urgent (threats of violence, child safety, immediate harm), report inside the app **and** email us at **hello@punkytigerlabs.com** so we can escalate faster.

## Appeals

If you believe we removed your content or suspended your account in error, email **hello@punkytigerlabs.com** within 30 days with your user ID and a description of the decision. We will review the decision and respond within 7 business days.

## Changes to these Guidelines

We may update these Guidelines from time to time. Material changes will be announced in-app and by email to account holders. Continued use of Flixscope after a change means you accept the updated Guidelines.

## Contact

Questions, feedback, or concerns: **hello@punkytigerlabs.com**
